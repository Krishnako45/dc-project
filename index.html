Data communication project

CRC error detection & data transmission simulator

Enter binary data and a generator polynomial. The app runs a real modulo-2 division to generate the CRC, simulates sending the frame, lets you introduce a transmission error, and shows the receiver detecting it.

Results will appear here after you calculate the CRC.

2. Transmission channel

Calculate the CRC first, then send the frame through the channel.

3. Receiver

Waiting for a frame to arrive from the channel.
